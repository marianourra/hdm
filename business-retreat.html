About the Retreat

Experience the perfect blend of adventure, team building, and professional growth at the world's longest wave.


Our exclusive corporate retreat in Chicama, Peru, is designed for forward-thinking tech companies and startups seeking to strengthen their team dynamics while enjoying one of the most unique surfing destinations on the planet.

At Chicama Corporate Retreat, we've created a unique environment where the power of nature meets professional development. Our program combines:


  • World-class surfing at the longest left-hand wave (2.2km)
  • Expert-led leadership and mindfulness workshops
  • Luxury accommodations with ocean views

Whether you're looking to boost team morale, develop leadership skills, or simply provide your team with an unforgettable experience, our retreat offers the perfect setting for growth and connection.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the retreat package? ▼
Our premium package includes luxury accommodations, daily surf lessons, equipment rental, all meals, airport transfers, team-building activities, and exclusive access to our private beach area.
Do I need to be an experienced surfer? ▼
Not at all! We welcome all skill levels. Our expert instructors will provide personalized coaching to ensure everyone has a great experience, whether you're a beginner or advanced surfer.
What's the best time to visit? ▼
The surf season in Chicama runs year-round, but the best conditions are typically from March to December. The weather is consistently warm and sunny, with water temperatures ranging from 18-22°C (64-72°F).
How do we handle team activities? ▼
We customize all team activities based on your group's size and preferences. Activities can include surf competitions, team challenges, cultural experiences, and professional development workshops.
What about safety and medical support? ▼
Safety is our top priority. We have certified lifeguards on duty, first-aid trained staff, and access to local medical facilities. We also provide travel insurance and 24/7 support throughout your stay.
